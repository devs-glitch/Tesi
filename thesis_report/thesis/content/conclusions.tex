\chapter{Discussion and Conclusions}
\label{ch:conclusions}

\section{The instrument}

This thesis set out to measure whether a quantized spiking neural network
reasons like its full-precision parent, and first found that the measurement
could not be interpreted until the instrument was calibrated.

A four-block convolutional spiking network classifies four families of transient
noise (\textit{glitches}) in LIGO data at $0.9833 \pm 0.0014$ validation
accuracy over three seeds, under constraints that keep the configuration
deployable by construction: a shift-implementable membrane decay, at most eight
time steps, and a 112-pixel input. None of the constraints costs measurable
accuracy, and the resolution choice lowered the dead fraction of the deepest
layer by $12.7$ percentage points.

The Spike Activation Map was implemented, its summation range corrected, and its
free parameter fixed on the temporal axis it governs instead of on
time-aggregated maps that cannot see it. Its faithfulness was tested
quantitatively: on the reference checkpoint it holds on two classes of four, with
a geometric interpretation for the other two, and across the three parents on
seven of the twelve class-and-seed cells.

The calibration established four levels of divergence: weight noise as large as
an entire 8-bit quantization step (PCC $0.975$ in the worst class), continued
training with no bits removed (PCC $0.987$ and IoU $0.750$ on average), a
thirtyfold change in the explainer (PCC $0.960$), and initialization alone (PCC
$0.153$). The last is far larger than expected, and it shaped the rest of the
study.

\paragraph{Spatial metrics cannot compare independently trained models.} Two
networks that agree to within one validation sample in accuracy produce
explanations correlating at $0.153$. The mechanism is a representational
permutation rather than a defect of the metric.

\paragraph{They can compare a model with its own parent.} Weight noise as large
as an entire 8-bit step, at least 26 times smaller than the 2-bit step of any
channel, leaves the PCC above $0.975$ in every class. A divergence beyond the
floor of its class is therefore a change in the explanation, not noise of the
instrument. The two results answer different questions, which is why every
quantized model is fine-tuned from, and compared with, its own parent.

\paragraph{Resulting figures are not interpretable without these levels.} Values
around $0.85$ are presented in the literature as evidence of preserved
explanations; for this architecture that value falls between the numerical floor
and the independent-solutions level, so it cannot be read without knowing which
comparison produced it. The floor and the independent-solutions level need no
training beyond a multi-seed baseline, which is good practice regardless; the
fine-tuning level needs one run per seed with the quantizer left out.

\paragraph{The class sensitivity ordering is a property of the class within a
network.} Changing the kernel and perturbing the weights, the two interventions
that act on a fixed network, rank the classes identically: Violin\_Mode, then
Scattered\_Light, Blip and Extremely\_Loud. A change of initialization does not
preserve the ordering, and neither does 2-bit quantization, where Blip loses
most. Extremely\_Loud, saturated and spatially extensive, registers no change
under any calibration, yet moves once the membrane is quantized to 2 bits.

The preparation of the membrane arm added a result of its own: the network makes
no use of negative membrane potentials. Clipping the hidden state to $[0, 2]$,
which removes up to 47 thresholds of negative range, costs one validation sample
and leaves the dynamics unchanged, so the membrane can be stored on an unsigned
grid. At 8 and 4 bits that saves one bit against a symmetric grid; at 2 bits it
keeps the one level between rest and threshold that a symmetric grid would lose.

\section{Quantization and explanation fidelity}

\paragraph{Accuracy is not a sufficient criterion for spiking networks.}
The finding of Rabbi et al.~\cite{rabbi2026} on convolutional networks carries
over, and in a sharper form. Two configurations of this network classify equally
well, a 4-bit weight model and a 2-bit membrane model, and their explanations
differ by a factor of almost two in agreement with the original. Selected on
accuracy alone, the second could be deployed: a model that reaches the right
labels while its highlighted region overlaps the original's with an IoU of
$0.438$. In the detector-characterization setting of
Chapter~\ref{ch:introduction} that is the scenario that matters. How deep the
change goes depends on the class: on the one model measured at several mask
sizes, the most salient positions of a Blip survive and the change lies in the
region around them, while for Violin\_Mode it reaches the most salient positions
as well.

\paragraph{The divergence is present but bounded.} The changed explanations are
still faithful. Deleting the pixels they highlight degrades the model's
confidence almost as much as deleting the parent's highlights does, a median of
$94\,\%$ of the parent's gap, and the quantized model can be explained with its
parent's maps as well as with its own. The quantized network has not stopped
using spectrogram evidence; among several redundant sets of evidence it now
highlights a partly different one. A faithfulness test alone would have declared
the explanation intact and an overlap test alone would have suggested it was
lost: two valid metrics measuring different properties of the same map.

\paragraph{The hypothesis was wrong in its form and right in its mechanism.}
The prediction that the membrane would move the temporal axis of the explanation
before the weights moved its spatial axis is not supported: both arms first
cross the floor and the fine-tuning level of every class at 4 bits, and at 2
bits the membrane changes where the explanation points more than when. What the
membrane does change first is the temporal dynamics of the network itself. At 2
bits, with a single representable level between rest and threshold, every
first-layer neuron that fires at least three times within an image does so at
exactly regular intervals, in all six models with a 2-bit membrane. The temporal
centre of mass, built to capture the time axis of the explanation, is blind to
this, because it measures where the spike mass falls and not how it is spaced. A
temporal explanation metric for spiking networks should see regularity as well
as timing, and the present instrument does not.

\paragraph{The comparison with Smith et al.\ inverts for the membrane.} Smith et
al.~\cite{smith2026} report that the membrane needs at least four bits, losing
$4$--$8\,\%$ of accuracy at two, while learned weight quantization remains
viable at two bits. The weight result agrees with theirs: a uniform 2-bit grid
scaled to the largest weight of each channel collapses the classifier here, as
their uniform quantizer with a wide clipping range collapses on CIFAR-100. The
membrane result does not: with a grid fitted to the measured range, a 2-bit
membrane keeps accuracy at parity.

\paragraph{Weak events are not where the explanation fails.} Quantization was
expected to disturb spike decisions mostly in weak events, whose potentials sit
near threshold. The data give no consistent support: at 2 bits the IoU gradient
with event strength is $+0.233$, $-0.007$ and $+0.059$ on the three seeds, a
spread as large as the effect. The one consistent effect has the opposite sign:
the strongest Extremely\_Loud events lose their explanation most, in all six
measurements. A plausible reading is that their evidence covers most of the
image, so the highlighted region is the least constrained.

\paragraph{Efficiency and explainability do not come to a trade off.} The
configuration that changes the explanation at constant accuracy is the
2-bit membrane, and it is the one that saves no energy: with weights in full
precision the per-operation cost stays the same while firing rates rise, at 2
bits by $11\,\%$ beyond what fine-tuning alone adds. The deployable
configuration, joint 4-bit, keeps accuracy at parity and an explanation that
moves only slightly beyond what continued training alone produces, at about one
sixtieth of the estimated energy and one eighth of the memory.

\paragraph{Seed variance is the dominant source of variation.} Three
full-precision networks of equal accuracy differ by a factor of $2.6$ in the
firing rate of their first layer, by more than half in estimated energy ($50.0$
to $78.1\,\mu$J), and in which classes SAM can explain faithfully at all. The
2-bit membrane IoU ranges from $0.26$ to $0.62$ across seeds, a spread as large
as any effect measured within a seed.

\section{Limitations}

\paragraph{Generalization of the levels.} The four calibration levels are
specific to this architecture, this dataset and this explainer. The
\emph{mechanism} behind the collapse between independently trained networks,
representational permutation, is general and well documented; its
\emph{magnitude} is not, and a different architecture could place the levels
elsewhere. What generalizes is the claim that the levels must be measured, not
the values measured here.

\paragraph{Labels are model output.} Accuracy is agreement with the Gravity Spy
convolutional classifier, whose output the labels are, and not with a physical
ground truth. A model that reproduced that classifier's systematic errors would
score well here. The per-class confidences are released with the labels and
would allow accuracy to be stratified by the reference classifier's own
certainty, which was not done.

\paragraph{$T$ sits at the imposed ceiling.} The search was forbidden from
exploring more than eight time steps, so it is not known whether it would have
preferred a longer integration window. An unconstrained search, at 224\,px,
selected seven, which bounds the likely cost without removing the question.

\paragraph{The deletion metric does not separate SAM from random everywhere.} On
the reference checkpoint it fails on Scattered\_Light and Violin\_Mode, and
across the three parents on five of the twelve class-and-seed cells. The
geometric explanation offered in Section~\ref{sec:faithfulness}, that with
extended, redundant evidence the region SAM ranks highest is not necessary
because the rest of the pattern still suffices, is an interpretation and not a
measurement. Deletion tests whether the highlighted pixels are necessary; the
insertion curve of the same work~\cite{petsiuk2018rise}, which starts from the
mean image and restores pixels in order of attributed importance, tests whether
they are sufficient. If the interpretation holds, insertion should separate SAM
from random order on the classes where deletion does not.

\paragraph{The explanation is class-agnostic.} SAM, as formulated and as
implemented here, uses no labels, so the deletion metric measures faithfulness
to the model's \emph{predicted} class. This is the correct reading of the metric,
but it makes misclassified examples informative, and they should be inspected
separately rather than pooled.

\paragraph{The temporal metric is partly kernel arithmetic.} A thirtyfold change
of $\gamma$ moves the centre of mass by $0.83$ to $0.93$ steps whatever the spike
trains, the amount a fully firing train produces (Section~\ref{sec:gamma}).
Between models compared at the same $\gamma$ this component cancels, so the
centre of mass carries information only as a difference between models, which
is the only form in which it is reported.

\paragraph{Quantization is simulated.} Fake quantization reproduces the values an
integer implementation would hold, but not its arithmetic: the membrane is
quantized as a stored state after each spike decision, while on integer hardware
the comparison with threshold would itself be made on integers. The energy
figures are analytical, at a 45\,nm reference node, and below eight bits they
rest on an extrapolation of the per-operation costs.

\paragraph{The overlap is read at a single $k$.} Every IoU of the grid is
computed at $k = 0.20$, the value of Rabbi et al.~\cite{rabbi2026}. The sweep
over $k$ exists for one model only, the 2-bit membrane of seed 3456, and there
the class ranking reverses between $k = 0.02$ and $k = 0.20$: whether the
headline overlap measures the most salient evidence or the ranking of the
region around it is settled for that model alone.

\paragraph{Sample sizes of the explanation analyses.} Divergence and the
fine-tuning level are measured on twenty validation images per class; the
numerical floor, the independent-solutions level, the $\gamma$ analysis and
faithfulness on fifteen; stratification on fifteen per class and tercile. The
numerical floor and the membrane range come from a single checkpoint. These sizes
suffice for the effects reported here, which are large, but limit the resolution
with which small differences between configurations can be ranked.

\paragraph{The fine-tuning level is not matched in epochs.} The control arm is
measured after its last epoch, six to fourteen epochs from the parent, while each
quantized model is the checkpoint selected at the epoch of its best validation
accuracy, which can come earlier. The level therefore bounds what continued
training does to an explanation, but it is not the effect of exactly the epochs
each quantized model received.

\paragraph{Checkpoints are selected on the partition on which they are
analyzed.} Selection uses accuracy and the analysis uses explanations, so the
dependence is weak, but it exists; the test-set evaluation remains the only
fully independent estimate.

\paragraph{The saving factor rests on an assumption.} Costing the analog first
convolution at the weight bit-width gives a joint 4-bit saving of $59$; costing
it in floating point gives $3.5$, between $2.8$ and $4.2$ across seeds. The
operation counts, the memory and the ranking of configurations do not depend on
it.

\section{Future work}

\paragraph{Deployment.} Hardware deployment was left out of scope. Exporting the
selected integer configuration to a neuromorphic platform or a microcontroller
would measure the latency, energy and memory that are estimated analytically
here.

\paragraph{Normalization.} The baseline contains no batch normalization, because
quantizing its parameters is a problem distinct from quantizing weights and
potentials. Deeper spiking networks need it, and it can be approximated with
shifts or folded into the preceding convolution before deployment; repeating the
study on a normalized architecture would test whether the conclusions survive.

\paragraph{The temporal axis of the input.} Under direct encoding every temporal
structure is generated by the network. Feeding the spectrogram column by column,
so that time in the input corresponds to time in the signal, would give the
temporal explanation a physical meaning.

\paragraph{Wider benchmark.} Extending the four classes to the full Gravity Spy
taxonomy, with a split by observing run, would make the classification results
comparable with the published benchmark.

\section{Closing remark}

Compressed models are increasingly deployed where their explanations are acted
upon. This thesis asked whether a quantized spiking network preserves the
internal dynamics and reasoning of the network it came from, and found that the
question has two prerequisites: calibrating the comparison metrics against the
values they report in the absence of any real change, and comparing every model
with its own parent. With both in place, the answer for this network is that it
largely does down to four bits in every arm, where explanations move about as
far as continued training moves them. The one configuration that does not, the
2-bit membrane, shows no sign of it in accuracy.

Correlation and the deletion test register the change only weakly: a PCC of
$0.923$ would still read as a preserved explanation, and the quantized model's
own maps keep a median $94\,\%$ of the parent's deletion gap. The change is plain
only in the overlap of the highlighted regions, an IoU of $0.438$, below the
floor of every class ($0.70$ to $0.84$).

A deployment chosen on accuracy alone could have picked this configuration, and
that is why the question was worth asking.
