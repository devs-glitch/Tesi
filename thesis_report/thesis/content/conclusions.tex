\chapter{Discussion and Conclusions}
\label{ch:conclusions}

\section{The instrument}

This thesis set out to measure whether a quantized spiking neural network
reasons like its full-precision parent, but first found that the measurement could not
be interpreted until the instrument was calibrated.

A four-block convolutional spiking network classifies four families of transient
noise (\textit{glitches} in LIGO data at $0.9833 \pm 0.0014$ 
validation accuracy over three seeds,
under constraints that keep the configuration deployable by
construction: a shift-implementable membrane decay, at most eight time
steps, and a 112-pixel input. Each of those constraints was
shown to cost nothing measurable in accuracy, and the resolution choice improved
the health of the deepest layer by 12.7 percentage points of dead fraction.

The Spike Activation Map was implemented, its summation range corrected, and its
free parameter fixed on the temporal axis it governs instead of the
time-aggregated maps that cannot see it. Its faithfulness was tested
quantitatively and found to hold on two classes of four, with a geometric
explanation for the other two.

The calibration of the instruments 
established three levels of divergence: the one produced by weight noise
as large as an entire 8-bit quantization step (PCC $0.975$), by a thirtyfold
change in the explainer (PCC $0.960$), and by initialization alone (PCC $0.153$).
The latter is far larger than expected, and imposed a few consequences.

\paragraph{Spatial metrics cannot compare independently trained models.}
Two networks that agree to within one validation sample in accuracy, indeed, produce
explanations correlating at $0.153$. The mechanism under this effect is a representational
permutation rather than any defect of the metric. Also, the disagreement is uniform
across samples so not driven by outliers.

\paragraph{They can compare a model with its own parent.} Under a perturbation 85
times smaller than the 2-bit quantization step, PCC remains above $0.975$,
meaning that the metric is stable. 
As a result a divergence beyond that floor is a change in the explanation, not noise
of the instrument. The two results answer different questions, and the
distinction should be made explicitly, which is why every quantized model is fine-tuned
from its own parent.

\paragraph{Resulting figures are not interpretable without these floors.} Values
around $0.85$ are presented in the literature as evidence of preserved
explanations, and for this architecture that value falls between the numerical floor
and the independent-solutions level, so it cannot be read without knowing which
comparison produced it. Establishing the floors requires no training runs beyond
a multi-seed baseline that is good practice regardless.

\paragraph{The class sensitivity ordering is
identical under three unrelated interventions.} Explanation sensitivity is
shown to not a property of the perturbation applied but to the specific
property of the class morphology, and
Extremely\_Loud --- saturated and spatially extensive --- registers no change
under any measure tested.

The preparation of the membrane arm added another 
notable result of its own, that is that the network makes
no use of negative membrane potentials. Clipping the hidden state to $[0, 2]$,
which removes up to 47 thresholds of negative range, costs one validation sample
and leaves the dynamics unchanged, so the membrane can be stored on an unsigned
grid at one bit less than a symmetric one would need.

\section{Quantization and explanation fidelity}

\paragraph{Accuracy is not a sufficient criterion for spiking networks.}
The finding of Rabbi et al.~\cite{rabbi2026} on convolutional networks carries
over, and in a sharper form. Two configurations of this network classify equally
well, a 4-bit weight model and a 2-bit membrane model, and their explanations
differ by a factor of almost two in agreement with the original. If the case was
to solely select a configuration based on accuracy, then the second would be deployed.
The caveat is that it is a model that reaches the right labels while showing a different part of the
spectrogram. In the detector-characterization setting of
Chapter~\ref{ch:introduction}, that is the scenario that matters: the label is
correct, the diagnostic cue is not the one the original model would have given.

\paragraph{The divergence is present but bounded.} The changed explanations are
still faithful: indeed deleting the pixels they highlight degrades the model's
confidence almost as much as deleting the parent's highlights does, and the
quantized model can be explained with its parent's maps as well as with its own.
Moreover, the quantized network has not stopped using spectrogram evidence, but among
several redundant sets of evidence it now highlights a different one. A
faithfulness test alone would have declared the explanation intact, while an overlap
test alone would have suggested it was lost, which is exaclty the symptom of two 
valid metrics that explain different dimentions of the same problem.

\paragraph{The hypothesis was wrong in its form and right in its mechanism.}
The prediction that the membrane would move the temporal axis of the explanation
before the weights moved its spatial axis is not supported: both arms first
register at 4 bits, and at 2 bits the membrane changes where the explanation
points more than when. What the membrane does change first is the temporal
dynamics of the network itself: at 2 bits, so with a single representable level
between rest and threshold, the first layer fires with perfectly regular
intervals. The temporal centre of mass, which built to capture the time axis of the
explanation, is blind to this, because it measures where the spike mass falls and
not how it is spaced. A temporal explanation metric for spiking networks should
see regularity as well as timing, and the present instrument does not.

\paragraph{The comparison with Smith et al.\ inverts.} Smith et
al.~\cite{smith2026} report that the membrane needs at least four bits while
learned weights survive two. The weight result agrees with theirs: 
uniform ternary weights fail, where they
needed a learned quantizer to keep two bits. The membrane result does not: with
a grid fitted to the measured range, a 2-bit membrane keeps accuracy at parity,
where they lose $4$--$8\,\%$.

\paragraph{Weak events are not where the explanation fails.} The 
expected behavior was that
quantization would disturb spike decisions mostly in weak events, whose
potentials sit near threshold. However this finds no consistent support: the gradient with
event strength changes sign across seeds. The only consistent effect is
reversed: the most saturated Extremely\_Loud events lose their explanation most,
because their evidence is spread over the whole image and the highlighted region
is least constrained.

\paragraph{Efficiency and explainability do not come to a trade off.} The
configuration that changes the explanation at constant accuracy is the
2-bit membrane, but it is the one that saves no energy: with weights in full
precision the per-operation cost stays the same while firing rates rise. The
deployable configuration, joint 4-bit, on the other hand,
keeps accuracy at parity and the
explanation at the numerical floor, at about one sixtieth of the estimated
energy and one eighth of the memory. The divergence between the three curves of
Figure~\ref{fig:headline} exists, but it occurs at a point not optimal
for efficiency.

\paragraph{Seed variance is the dominant source of variation.} Three
full-precision networks of equal accuracy differ by a factor of 2.6 in the
firing rate of their first layer, by more than half in estimated energy, and in
which classes SAM can explain faithfully at all (e.g. he 2-bit membrane IoU ranges
from $0.26$ to $0.62$ across seeds).

\section{Limitations}

\paragraph{Generalization of the thresholds.} The three calibration levels are
specific to this architecture, this dataset and this explainer. The
\emph{mechanism} behind the collapse, representational permutation between
independently trained networks, is general and well documented, but the
\emph{magnitude} is not and a different architecture could place the floors
at different levels. What generalizes is the claim that the floors must be measured, not
the values measured here.

\paragraph{Labels are model output.} Accuracy is agreement with the Gravity Spy
convolutional classifier, as the dataset labels are its output, and not
the physical ground truth. This means that a model that
reproduced that classifier's systematic errors would score well here. The
per-class confidences are available and would permit stratifying accuracy by the
reference classifier's own certainty, which was not done, but could indeed be
a valueable contribution.

\paragraph{$T$ sits at the imposed ceiling.} The search was forbidden from
exploring more than eight time steps, so it is not known whether it would have
preferred a longer integration window. Nonetheless, an unconstrained search selected seven,
which bounds the likely cost but does not completely eliminate the question.

\paragraph{The deletion metric does not separate SAM from random on two
classes.} The geometric explanation offered in Section~\ref{sec:faithfulness}
--- that with extended, redundant evidence the region SAM ranks highest is
and interpretation and it is not
necessary, because the rest of the pattern still suffices.
Deletion tests whether the highlighted
pixels are necessary; the insertion curve of the same
work~\cite{petsiuk2018rise}, which starts from the mean image and restores pixels
in order of attributed importance, tests whether they are sufficient. If the
interpretation holds, indeed, insertion should separate SAM from random order on
Scattered\_Light and Violin\_Mode where the deletion does not.

\paragraph{The explanation is class-agnostic.} Since SAM, as originally
formulated and accurately implemented, does not use labels, so the
deletion metric measures faithfulness to the model's \emph{predicted} class. This
is the correct reading of the metric, but it
means misclassified examples are informative and should be inspected separately
rather than pooled.

\paragraph{The temporal metric is partly kernel arithmetic.} The $\gamma$
analysis showed that the centre of mass of the SAM energy is dominated by the
minority of near-continuously firing neurons. Between models compared at the same
$\gamma$ this component cancels, but it means that the CoM is most sensitive to
changes in that minority.

\paragraph{Quantization is simulated.} Fake quantization reproduces the values an
integer implementation would hold, but not its arithmetic: the membrane is 
indeed quantized as a stored state after each spike decision, 
while on integer hardware the
comparison with threshold would itself be performed on integers. Moreover, the energy
figures are analytical, at a 45\,nm reference node, and below eight bits they
rest on an extrapolation of the per-operation costs.

\paragraph{Sample sizes of the explanation analyses.} Divergence is measured on
twenty validation images per class, faithfulness on ten, stratification on
fifteen per class and tercile, and the membrane range on a single checkpoint.
These resultes to be sufficient for the effects reported in this thesis, 
which are large, but limit the resolution with which 
small differences between configurations can be
ranked.

\paragraph{The control does not isolate the fine-tuning.} Fine-tuning without a
quantizer never improved the parent's validation accuracy, so the selected
control checkpoint is the parent itself. The control therefore shows that the
pipeline is inert, but not directly what the extra epochs alone would do to a model that
does change, but the quantized checkpoints combine both the effects.

\paragraph{Checkpoints are selected on the validation partition on which they
are then analyzed.} Even if the dependence is weak, With selection by 
accuracy and analysis of explanations, the
the test-set evaluation remains the only fully independent
estimate.

\paragraph{The saving factor rests on an assumption.} Costing the analog first
convolution at the weight bit-width gives a joint 4-bit saving of 62; costing it
in floating point gives 3.5. The operation counts, the memory and the ranking of
configurations do not depend on it.

\section{Future work}

\paragraph{Deployment.} Hardware deployment was left out of scope, but could be
the natural continuation, exporting the selected integer configuration to a
neuromorphic platform or a microcontroller, and measuring on it the latency,
energy and memory that are estimated analytically here.

\paragraph{Normalization.} The baseline contains no batch normalization, because
quantizing its parameters is a problem distinct from quantizing weights and
potentials. Nonetheless, deeper spiking networks need it, 
and both approximating it with shifts
and removing it altogether through progressive training are available. So repeating
the study on a normalized architecture would test whether the conclusions survive.

\paragraph{The temporal axis of the input.} Under direct encoding every temporal
structure is generated by the network, so feeding the spectrogram column by column,
so that time in the input corresponds to time in the signal, would give the
temporal explanation a further physical meaning.

\paragraph{Wider benchmark.} Extending the four classes to the full Gravity Spy
taxonomy, adding also a split by observing run, would make the classification results
comparable with the published benchmark.

\section{Closing remark}

Compressed models are increasingly deployed where their explanations are acted
upon. This thesis asked whether a quantized spiking network preserves
the internal dynamics and reasoning as the network it came from, 
and found that the question first has
two prerequisites: calibrating the comparison metrics against the values they
report in the absence of any real change, and comparing every model with its own
parent. With both assessed, the
answer for this network is that it mostly does --- down to four bits, in every
arm --- and that the one configuration that does not, the 2-bit membrane, shows no
sign of it in accuracy, which does not degrade accordingly. 

Correlation and the deletion test
register the change only weakly: a PCC of $0.923$ would still read as a
preserved explanation, and the quantized model's own maps keep a median $94\,\%$
of the parent's deletion gap. The plain and detectable change is only in the IoU, 
which is the metrics that studies 
which pixels a reader is actually shown: $0.438$, against a floor of $0.704$.

Ultimately, s deployment chosen on accuracy alone could have picked this configuration, and
that is why the question was worth asking.
